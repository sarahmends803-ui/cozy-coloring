TABLU - Your Little Cozy Coloring Corner   (Made by Tablu)

UPLOAD TO GITHUB (replace the old files):
1. Open your repository on GitHub, then Add file > Upload files.
2. Upload ALL 6 files from this folder: index.html, sw.js, manifest.webmanifest, icon-192.png, icon-512.png, icon-maskable-512.png.
   (Same names as before, so GitHub will replace the old ones. Do NOT type or edit sw.js by hand.)
3. Commit changes. Wait 2 minutes. Open your site in Chrome, reload twice.
4. Chrome menu (three dots) > Install app (or Add to Home screen).

To add your own pages for everyone, see pages/HOW-TO-ADD-PAGES.txt

Your old saved pictures and colors stay on the device. Install needs the https address GitHub Pages gives you.
Fonts and PDF import need internet the first time; everything else works offline after the first visit.

WHAT'S NEW (Precision Coloring)
- Fill and Color Inside Lines now find every tiny space: small petals, hearts, stars, thin shapes and narrow gaps.
- Paint reaches right up to the black outline, so no white edges are left, and it never jumps into a neighbouring shape.
- Zoom goes up to 1600% and brush sizes go down to 0.5 for tiny details. Strokes are smoothed when you zoom in.
- Pages are drawn at a higher resolution so small details are sharper.
- Your saved projects keep their colors and open as before.

WHAT'S NEW (Edge-to-edge brush + Smart Library)
- The Brush with Color Inside Lines now reaches the inner edge of soft or blurry black outlines, with no thin white gaps. Outlines stay black and clear.
- When you press I'm Finished, the colored page moves from Uploaded Pictures to Finished Coloring Pages by itself. Your original picture is kept safely, and it is only moved after the finished page is saved and checked.
- Finished pages have a new "Color again" button for a fresh copy. If you delete a finished page, the original picture returns to Uploaded Pictures.
- Pages you finished before this update move automatically too.
